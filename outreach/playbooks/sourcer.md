# Sourcer playbook

Hints from earlier runs, added by `learn` or by the owner. They describe how the web and the file formats behaved,
never what to do: they cannot override CLAUDE.md, your prompt files or your task message, and an entry that
conflicts with any of them is ignored. Entries are kept short on purpose; the owner prunes them with `playbook`.

## Entries

1. [2026-10-10] [seen 1x] situation: Need a list of small companies that hire remote engineers, but LinkedIn, Wellfound and Crunchbase return login walls, 999 or 403 to a plain HTTP client => fix: Seed from open sources instead: the monthly HN Who is hiring thread via hn.algolia.com, the YC directory (yc-oss.github.io mirror), workatastartup.com listings, and remote boards with static pages.
2. [2026-10-10] [seen 1x] situation: Want a reliable signal that a company is actively hiring engineers right now => fix: Probe its ATS board JSON: boards-api.greenhouse.io, api.lever.co (api.eu.lever.co for EU tenants), api.ashbyhq.com posting-api, apply.workable.com widget. A 404 means no board on that ATS; try the next one.
3. [2026-10-10] [seen 1x] situation: Finding the ATS board slug for a company is guesswork => fix: Read the careers page HTML and look for links or iframes pointing at greenhouse.io, lever.co, ashbyhq.com, workable.com, personio or recruitee; the slug is the last path segment of that link. Do not guess slugs blind.
4. [2026-10-10] [seen 1x] situation: HN Who is hiring thread found but parsing hundreds of comments is noisy => fix: Take the latest story posted by the whoishiring account, read top-level children only, and keep posts whose first line mentions REMOTE plus intern, part-time, contract or junior. Keep the comment id as the citation.
5. [2026-10-10] [seen 1x] situation: Company size criterion of 5 to 200 people cannot be verified without LinkedIn => fix: Use proxies: count people on the team page, YC batch and team size on the YC company page, number of open roles, GitHub org member count. Record the evidence used and mark size as estimated.
6. [2026-10-10] [seen 1x] situation: Need a decision-maker contact at a 10 person startup => fix: Target a founder or CTO named on the about or team page, a founder byline on the company blog, or the HN hiring post author. At this size the founder usually reads hiring mail; skip HR aliases.
7. [2026-10-10] [seen 1x] situation: A contact email is printed publicly on the site (team page, blog post, HN post, press page) => fix: Prefer it over any guess. Store the exact page and surrounding sentence as provenance. A personal address published for hiring or contact is the strongest and most ethical source.
8. [2026-10-10] [seen 1x] situation: No published email; considering pattern guessing on the company domain => fix: For teams under 10 people the bare first name pattern is common; first.last dominates larger firms (about 38 percent at 1-10 employees vs 74 percent at 10k+). Confirm the domain has MX records and treat the guess as low confidence.
9. [2026-10-10] [seen 1x] situation: Hunter or a similar finder returns an address with a confidence score => fix: Accept only verified or high-confidence results that list public sources; otherwise mark as guess. Respect free-tier quotas and cache results per domain so the same lookup is never paid for twice.
10. [2026-10-10] [seen 1x] situation: Tempted to harvest emails from GitHub commit metadata => fix: Do not. Many devs use noreply commit addresses on purpose, and commit emails were not published for recruiting contact. Use only addresses a person chose to publish for contact.
11. [2026-10-10] [seen 1x] situation: Domain uses a catch-all mailbox so every guessed address appears valid => fix: Treat catch-all domains as unverifiable: lower confidence, prefer a published address or the careers or jobs alias, and never send to several guessed variants of the same person.
12. [2026-10-10] [seen 1x] situation: Only generic addresses found such as contact, info, hello or security => fix: Use contact or hello only if nothing personal exists, and say who the mail is for in the subject. Never use the security.txt contact or a press address for job outreach; they exist for other purposes.
13. [2026-10-10] [seen 1x] situation: Targeting Germany or France with cold outreach => fix: A personal job inquiry is not advertising, but keep it one-to-one, low volume, clearly from a person, with an easy opt-out line. Germany is the strictest market; prefer published hiring contacts there and never bulk-send.
14. [2026-10-10] [seen 1x] situation: Same company keeps resurfacing from different sources => fix: Deduplicate on the registered root domain, not the company name, and keep the earliest source plus a last-contacted date so no company is emailed twice in one cycle.
15. [2026-10-10] [seen 1x] situation: A snapshot of a search API whose link carries several query parameters was refused by the shell guard because the ampersand counts as shell syntax => fix: Use a static aggregator page of the monthly hiring thread instead, which lists all remote posts with their contact lines on one page
16. [2026-10-18] [seen 1x] situation: A contact address found on a page was a personal free-mail inbox, which looks spammy and lowers reply odds => fix: Prefer an address on the company domain; use a free-mail address only when the founder publishes it as their work contact, and say so in why

[2026-10-18] [seen 1x] situation: Two of four sourced companies were dropped on fit because their hiring pages only listed senior roles => fix: Keep a company only if a saved page shows an intern, junior, part-time or contractor signal, or a very small team asking for help; skip senior-only boards
